## 0. RALPLAN summary

This is a broader initiative with one LARGE feature slice, one SMALL records-and-validator close slice, four SMALL decision-and-promotion slices, and one SMALL optional rider. The intended outcome is a reusable comparison surface that drives the real imperative overlay furniture in live canvas context, then uses that surface to close the four owner-held visual questions without guessing their values. Completion also closes the v3.5 bookkeeping debt, preserves the shipped default until each owner ruling is recorded, demonstrates every new gate RED, and leaves `.descvi/screens.json` byte-identical unless an explicitly reviewed story says otherwise.

### Principles

1. **Compare the mechanism that actually paints.** A facsimile is not evidence about imperative furniture.
2. **The shipped default is a gate subject, not a convenient preset.** Comparison overrides are inputs layered over it and cannot silently redefine it.
3. **A LOOK change is a contract change.** Prepare the contract and its RED proof before promoting a chosen look, following the discipline stated at `.omc/specs/v3-layout-panel-visual-spec.md#⚠ **Both are CONTRACT changes, not CSS changes.**`: a restyle commit that leaves a marked-universe gate asserting the old obligation is knowingly red, and one that silently widens that gate's exemption is drift. That clause sits in a section phase-46 S46-2 closed in place, so it is read here as standing discipline rather than as pending input.
4. **Owner values remain owner values.** The plan chooses the surface and sequence; it does not choose the winning visual values or the reorder threshold.
5. **No fixture census is paid without a new test subject.** Existing routes provide the live contexts, and no screen is authored for the comparison mechanism.

### Decision drivers

- **DR49-1 — Fidelity:** the surface must drive the same DOM writers that paint `#dsh-ring-layer`, not a React reconstruction.
- **DR49-2 — Contract isolation:** opening the surface without applying a candidate and Reset must not move the subjects of G47-3, G47-6, G46-2/G46-3, G42-7, G48-4a, or the default-style assertions; candidate-only states that exercise an explicitly prepared replacement contract remain isolated from the shipped profile. Widening a contract's declared VOCABULARY so a candidate has a legal state is not moving its subject, and is only isolation-preserving while the new member stays uninhabited in production and carries its own positive obligation. **That argument is about a TRANSITIONAL uninhabited member — one whose inhabiting promotion is the next story — and it does not extend to a terminal one.** A kind that never gets inhabited is a permanent hole in four closed sets and the spec KIND table, asserting nothing and licensing anything that declares it. So the landing carries its own exit: S49-1 lands it as one revert unit (c), and S49-4's stop branch reverts it if the promotion does not happen.
- **DR49-3 — Recurring cost:** the chosen home must avoid the authoring-census and identity-golden bill unless a story truly adds a screen or a collected browser row.

## 6. ADR

### Decision

Build a runtime overlay comparison surface in `packages/descvi/src/react/overlay/**`, driven by one typed profile threaded into the existing imperative painters. Use existing `src/app/**` routes only as live contexts. Keep the shipped profile unchanged through S49-1, then promote each owner-chosen look through a contract-first sequence. For the one-mark spacing candidate, use C49-4’s non-operable affordance-keyed mark and `delegated-mark` roots rather than preserving per-root glyph ownership. Land the `delegated-mark` vocabulary uninhabited in S49-1, one story ahead of the ruling that inhabits it. Archive the two v3 plan files and land the owner-pass record and its validator in S49-0. Take reorder threshold U3 only as an isolated optional rider whose value feeds all three reorder threshold consumers, press-scoped at the selection shield.

### Drivers

- The furniture is imperative and cannot be edited through a descvi-authored page.
- Owner decisions require actual canvas context rather than a detached facsimile.
- Fixture screens and collected browser tests carry recurring census/golden costs.

### Alternatives Considered

- A static gallery screen: lower code cost, insufficient mechanism fidelity.
- Promoted `lab/tests/**` fixtures: useful contexts, not a painter; high recurring census cost.
- A sandbox comparison screen: permits helper modules but is equally expensive for whole-corpus extraction and still cannot own overlay painting.
- Leaving all visual decisions routed: avoids new chrome but repeats the condition that already blocked them.

### Why Chosen

The runtime surface is the only option that compares the actual painter with live geometry and theme while avoiding a second renderer and a new screen. A profile seam also provides the minimum reversible path: inactive mode preserves current behavior, candidates remain ephemeral, and each owner ruling can be promoted independently.

### Consequences

- Overlay chrome gains a persistent comparison entry point and its tests.
- The painters gain explicit look inputs; default behavior must be protected by fixed oracles.
- Visual decisions become small, independently reversible promotions after one large surface story.
- A promoted one-mark spacing treatment expands the declared-kind vocabulary to `delegated-mark`, replaces per-root glyph/visibility obligations with an exact-one non-operable mark per affordance, and leaves strip/apron hit ownership intact. It also retires two G44-5 universe-B legs whose subject is the per-root mark — the published size term and the painted/hidden inhabitation pair — under AGENTS.md's first deletion reason, each with a named replacement carrying its own RED. The vocabulary lands one story earlier than the promotion, in S49-1 and uninhabited, because the candidate has no legal state under the shipped KIND contract otherwise — the certain proof of that being the spacing family's own closed-set and kind-pin legs — and it lands in five hand-written closed sets and every production/spec closed-set restatement at once, the layer-wide G42-7 universe included, since none of them may be derived from another. The spacing-family KIND PINS are a separate list on a separate schedule — they say what spacing roots ARE, so the promotion falsifies them and the vocabulary does not, and C49-4 re-derives both lists by grep rather than carrying an enumeration.
- Whether the layer-wide G42-7 universe ever sees a shipped delegated root is unmeasured today and is settled by EG49-5 before S49-1 writes that arm. The arm is written and planted either way; only the claim that it covers real roots waits on the census.
