# Requirements Document Template

Use `Approved` only after explicit consolidated approval and when no blocking decision remains. Use `Draft` when the user requests an artifact with a blocking gap, add `## Blocking gaps`, and leave the active ledger resumable.

```markdown
# <Title>

Status: Approved | Draft
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

<Why this work exists and the existing situation it changes.>

## Desired outcome

<Observable end state without an implementation plan.>

## Scope

### In scope

- <required outcome>

### Out of scope

- <explicit non-goal>

## Requirements

- <behavioral or product requirement>

## Constraints

- <technical, operational, business, or compatibility limit>

## Decision boundaries

- <decision an implementer may make without returning to the user>

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] <testable or observable completion criterion>

## Decisions and rationale

- **<decision>:** <choice and reason>

## Relevant system evidence

- `<path or symbol>`: <descriptive fact that shaped the requirements>

## Assumptions and risks

- <assumption, uncertainty, or material risk>

## Deferred items

- <explicit deferral and the later gate that owns it>
```
